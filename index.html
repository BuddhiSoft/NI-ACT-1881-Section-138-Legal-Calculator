Section 138 calculation logic

  1. Cheque presentation is checked against the applicable three-month bank validity period.
  2. After information regarding dishonour is received, statutory notice must be issued within 30 days.
  3. The drawer gets 15 days from receipt/service of notice to make payment.
  4. The cause of action arises after expiry of those 15 days if payment is not made.
  5. Section 142 provides the one-month complaint limitation from the cause-of-action date, subject to statutory condonation.

Actual Service vs Presumption

Actual Notice Served

Use the date on which the drawer actually received the statutory notice when established.

Presumption Date

Use the date on which service is legally presumed on the facts. A correct address and proper postal dispatch can attract Section 27 of the General Clauses Act; the calculator does not invent a universal postal-day number.

C.C. Alavi Haji v. Palapetty Muhammed recognizes a rebuttable presumption of service where notice is properly addressed and sent by post.
